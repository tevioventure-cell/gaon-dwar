About GaonDwar

Rooted in a farming family in Uttar Pradesh.

GaonDwar began with a simple question: why do the most authentic things from our villages reach cities without their name, their story, or fair value for the people who make them?

We started from the village itself — the farms, the kaarigars and the traditions of UP — and built a brand that carries all three together. Every product we offer says where it comes from, and every tradition we share is explained the way it is practised at home.

What we do

Three things, done together.

01

Puja samagri & kits

Complete puja kits prepared as per vidhi — Satyanarayan Katha, Diwali Lakshmi-Ganesh Pujan, Navratri Ghatasthapana and Chhath.

02

Village crafts

Handicrafts from the artisan clusters of Uttar Pradesh, credited to the district and craft they come from. Village food products will follow.

03

Culture & content

Puja vidhi, process films, lok geet and bhajans — documenting how traditions are actually practised in the villages of UP.

Named by the place it comes from

We follow the district-plus-product way of naming that India has always used. The name tells you the origin, and the origin tells you the quality.
